What do these methods mean? ⓘ

CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) — start-to-end growth, ignoring the path between.
Formula: (end / start)(1/N) − 1. 5Y CAGR for year 2025 uses values from 2020 and 2025 (6 data points: 2020-2025). 2Y CAGR for 2025 uses values from 2023 and 2025 (3 data points: 2023, 2024, 2025).

Slope — least-squares linear regression across all 6 years (2020-2025), normalized by the mean absolute value. Captures direction even when start & end happen to be similar.

+slope checkbox — combine the main filter with a positive-slope requirement. Example: "CAGR ≥ 5% and trend is positive". Has no effect if the chosen method is already Slope.

Threshold is the comparison value (0.05 = 5%). Combined with Direction (Up/Down): Up keeps rows above threshold; Down keeps rows below.
